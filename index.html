Call Me Ish

Ishuah Kariuki is a husband, father, software engineer, and SRE. He graduated from Kenyatta University in 2011 with a BSc. in Computer Science. He's worked with several startups in the Kenyan tech ecosystem and most recently at Microsoft.

Ishuah is currently exploring AI safety and alignment, as well as other paths at the intersection of humans and AI.

Ishuah prefers the short form moniker "Ish". He's available to chat about various topics, a game of chess, or a run around Nairobi. You can reach out to him on X @ishuah_, collaborate on Github, or contact him by email at kariuki@ishuah.com.
